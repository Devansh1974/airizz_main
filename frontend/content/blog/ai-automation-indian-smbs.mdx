## The Problem With "Starting With AI"

Most Indian SMBs get sold on the idea of AI and then get overwhelmed by where to begin. Should you build a chatbot? Automate your marketing? Implement predictive analytics? The answer depends entirely on where you're losing the most time and money right now.

## Step 1: Find Your Biggest Manual Bottleneck

Before touching any AI tool, spend 2 hours this week asking your team one question: "What task do you do most often that feels like it shouldn't require a human?" The answers will point to your highest-ROI automation opportunity.

Common answers from Indian SMBs we've worked with:
- Manual data entry between CRM and accounting software
- Following up on unpaid invoices
- Responding to repetitive customer queries (WhatsApp, email)
- Generating weekly/monthly reports from spreadsheets

## Step 2: Quantify the Cost

Multiply: (hours spent per week) × (average hourly cost of the person doing it) × 52. Most SMBs are surprised to find they're spending ₹5–20L annually on tasks that could be 80% automated.

## Step 3: Start With Integration, Not Intelligence

The biggest mistake SMBs make is jumping to "AI" when they actually need "integration." If your CRM and accounting software don't talk to each other, no amount of machine learning will fix that. Fix the data pipeline first.

## Step 4: Pick One Workflow and Automate It Fully

Don't try to automate everything at once. Pick one workflow — ideally the most painful one — and automate it end-to-end. Get one win before expanding.
