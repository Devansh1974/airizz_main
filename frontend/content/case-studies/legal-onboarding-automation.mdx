## The Challenge
A Bangalore legal firm was onboarding new clients through a manual process: email chains, physical document collection, manual data entry into their case management system. Each onboarding took 14 working days and 40+ staff hours.

## Our Approach
AIRIZZ designed an automated onboarding workflow integrating document collection, KYC verification, and case management system population into a single client-facing portal.

**What we built:**
- Digital client intake portal
- Automated KYC document validation
- CRM + case management integration
- Automated welcome sequence and status notifications

## The Results
- Client onboarding reduced from 14 days to 2 days
- 40 staff hours saved per new client onboarding
- Client NPS improved by 32 points (clients cited faster onboarding as top driver)
- Firm expanded client capacity by 40% without adding headcount

"We didn't realise how much time we were losing to paperwork until it was gone. AIRIZZ automated what we thought was impossible to automate." — Managing Partner
