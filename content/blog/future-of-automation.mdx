AI is no longer just for global tech giants. Traditional Indian small and medium enterprises (SMEs) are leveraging tailored AI systems to automate operations, scale client communication, and save on resource costs.

### Why Off-the-Shelf AI Fails

Generic models lack context about:
- Local business dynamics and pricing schemes.
- WhatsApp-first buyer workflows.
- Internal SOP databases and product catalogs.

By tailoring LLMs specifically to company documentation (RAG systems) and connecting them to transaction engines, SMEs can automate over 80% of routine client interactions securely.

### The Power of Integration

Connecting your email, Google Sheets, ERP, and WhatsApp enables:
1. **Instant Billing verification:** Customers receive invoices right inside chat.
2. **24/7 Support:** AI answering queries about product catalog availability.
3. **Automated Follow-ups:** Churn prediction algorithms alerts.
