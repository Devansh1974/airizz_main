Sharma Logistics faced severe bottlenecks in tracking customer inquiries across multiple corporate phone numbers. Leads were getting lost in private threads, and sales staff lacked context.

### The Solution

We implemented a unified WhatsApp CRM sync pipeline:
1. **Webhook Ingestion:** Catching inbound text messages via the WhatsApp Business API.
2. **Context Routing:** Scoring client priority based on historical interaction patterns.
3. **Automated Alerts:** Syncing client profiles instantly to an internal hub for the sales team.

### The Outcome

By organizing buyer pipelines and configuring auto-responders for catalog requests, the business recovered lost opportunities and streamlined inventory bookings.
