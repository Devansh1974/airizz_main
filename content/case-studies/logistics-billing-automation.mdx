## The Challenge
A Mumbai-based logistics firm was manually reconciling invoices from 4 different carrier systems with their internal billing platform. The process took 3 full working days per month and had a 12% error rate — costing the business ₹18L+ annually in billing disputes.

## Our Approach
AIRIZZ built an automated data integration pipeline connecting all 4 carrier APIs to the client's billing platform. We implemented automated reconciliation logic, exception flagging for anomalies, and a real-time dashboard for the finance team.

**What we built:**
- Custom API connectors for 4 carrier systems
- Automated reconciliation engine with rule-based exception handling
- Finance dashboard with real-time billing status
- Email alert system for flagged discrepancies

## The Results
- Billing errors reduced by 62% in the first month
- Monthly reconciliation now completes overnight (vs. 3 working days)
- Finance team reclaimed 22 hours per month for higher-value work
- ₹18L in annual billing dispute costs eliminated
